04Brief Introduction

Yi-Han Chen received the B.S., M.S., and Ph.D. degrees in electrical engineering from the National University of Kaohsiung, Kaohsiung, Taiwan, in 2011, 2013, and 2025, respectively. Before his doctoral studies, he co-founded Laevateinn, Kaohsiung, Taiwan, where he served as a Web Systems Engineer in 2014, and subsequently joined the Artificial Intelligence Research Center at the National University of Kaohsiung in 2018. He is currently an Assistant Professor in the Department of Industrial Management at National Pingtung University of Science and Technology, Taiwan, and a Consultant at AGROUPS Inc. in Taiwan. His current research interests include generative AI, AI-AOI, smart manufacturing, and robotics.

01實驗室簡介

智慧製造視覺與機器人實驗室（MIRA Lab）

Manufacturing, IoT, Robotics & AI Laboratory · Grounded AI for the Factory Floor

本實驗室將物聯網（IoT）、AI 與機器人（Robotics）導入製造（Manufacturing）現場，聚焦 AI-AOI 缺陷檢測、生成式影像資料擴增與模型漂移檢測，並延伸至機器人視覺取物與避障、智慧排程、預知保養，以及 AI Agent 與 RAG 落地工廠知識庫。

MIRA 取自四個字首：Manufacturing、IoT、Robotics、AI；在西班牙文中 mira 意為「看」，呼應以視覺為核心的研究。
